MV.ai V4 — Vision Input v0.1 Patch
==================================

1. Fully close MV.ai.
2. Make a backup of your working MV.ai_V4 folder.
3. Extract this ZIP.
4. Copy everything INSIDE MV.ai_V4_IMAGE_ANALYSIS_PATCH into your working MV.ai_V4 folder.
5. Choose "Replace files in the destination".
6. Do not delete or replace your .env, .venv, user_profile.json, or AppData database.
7. Run TEST_IMAGE_SUPPORT.bat.
8. Start MV.ai with RUN_MVAI.bat.

The visible attachment button is named exactly: ADD Stuff!

This patch adds image analysis only. Image generation and editing are not included yet.
