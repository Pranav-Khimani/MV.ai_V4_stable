MV.ai V4 — More / Settings Popup
================================

1. Fully close MV.ai.
2. Copy the ui folder from this patch into your current MV.ai_V4 folder.
3. Choose "Replace files in the destination".
4. Keep .env, .venv and user_profile.json untouched.
5. Start MV.ai with RUN_MVAI.bat.

The More button now opens a centered, dimmed, medium-sized control panel.
Top dock tabs:
- General
- Profile
- Voice
- Data & privacy
- About

Click outside the panel, press Escape, or use × to close it.
